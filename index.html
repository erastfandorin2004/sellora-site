What the app does

Infographics

A marketplace-style product card with callouts, benefits and specs — generated from your photo.

AI Photoshoot

Your product in a styled scene or interior — like a studio shoot, without the studio.

White background

How it works

1. Upload a product photo and pick a mode
Infographics, AI photoshoot, white background or free mode.
2. The app generates a sheet of 4 images
One generation = one sheet of 4 images. Generation usually takes a few minutes, with automatic quality checks along the way.
3. Save and publish
Download the results to your phone and use them in your marketplace listings. You can use the images commercially.
